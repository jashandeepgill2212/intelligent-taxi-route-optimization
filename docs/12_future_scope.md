# Chapter 12: Future Scope & Enhancements

## 12.1 Planned Enhancements
1. **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**: Integrate Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) or Graph Attention Networks (GAT) to embed non-Euclidean road network topologies directly into state representations.
2. **Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL)**: Extend single-agent DQN to multi-agent taxi fleet coordination (using MADDPG or QMIX) to avoid collective traffic congestion bottlenecks.
3. **Live GPS & Sensor Integration**: Connect real-time telematics and live OpenStreetMap Overpass traffic feeds for production deployment.
